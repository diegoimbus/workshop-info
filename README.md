# Workshop info

Join links for the Medellín workshop sessions.

## How to join

1. Open the page for your session:
   - [Medellín 1](medellin-1/)
   - [Medellín 2](medellin-2/)
   - [Medellín 3](medellin-3/)
2. Find your team in the table, by number or by name.
3. Click **Join** and sign in with your email.

The **Join** link won't work? Go to <https://catalog.us-east-1.prod.workshops.aws/join> and type your team's **access code**.

> Only use your own team's link. Each link opens that team's AWS workspace.
